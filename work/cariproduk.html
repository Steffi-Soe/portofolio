Case study · 2025

From "45 DEG ELBOW, 3000 LB, SOCW" to the right catalog item

Cariproduk takes customer requests for piping parts, whether typed, photographed or sent as a spreadsheet, and finds the matching item in the company's catalog.

My role
Built it end to end
When
Jan – Dec 2025
Status
Delivered, checked every month
Built with
Gemini API, Ollama, FAISS, Meilisearch, FastAPI

The problem

Every customer describes the same part differently. One writes "SOCW", another "SW", a third "socket weld". Sizes come in millimetres or inches, pressure classes as "3000 LB" or "3000#", and the material grade might be anywhere in the line. Staff matched each line to the catalog by hand.

What it has to read

How it works

Flanges get special handling. A table of interchangeable flanges across DIN, JIS and ANSI standards lets a request in one standard match an equivalent item listed under another.

How I measured it

Most months I ran a batch of real requests through it and compared the result with what was actually quoted or sold. That turned out to be harder than it sounds: sometimes sales quoted a different grade or connection than the customer asked for, so "what was sold" isn't always the right answer. Disagreements went to a manual review.

Accuracy rose from about 90% in April 2025 to 97.6% in September, or 97.1% if the lines sent to manual review count as misses. The target was 80%.

Where it still trips up

  • Wall thickness written as measurements. A request like "Φ114.3×6.02" gives the outside diameter and wall thickness in millimetres. It has to be translated into a schedule, and those lines still often need a check.
  • Grade mix-ups. In some lines sales quoted 316 stainless where the request said 304. Cases like that are hard to grade automatically, so they went to manual review.
  • Items the catalog doesn't carry. For some JIS flanges the right answer is "no match". Returning the closest wrong item would be worse.
