2026 · LIVE SINCE AUG 2026 · ON-PREMISE

ACS Knowledge Assistant

IT policies, SOPs and budget data were spread across SharePoint, and finding one answer took about 8 minutes. Now staff ask in chat and get a cited answer, or a clear "not in the documents".

  • Hybrid search in Qdrant (multilingual-e5 + BM25, RRF fusion), with access control applied at retrieval, not in the UI.
  • Figures come from a DuckDB text-to-SQL lane, so the model never guesses a number.
  • Runs entirely on local LLMs and one RTX 4060 GPU; no data leaves the company.

02 · APPROACH

Less demo, more deployment

Evaluate before claiming
