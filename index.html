Selected work

2026 · In use since August

ACS Knowledge Assistant

Our IT team's policies, SOPs and budget sheets were spread across SharePoint, and finding one answer took about eight minutes. Now people ask in a chat window and get an answer with its sources, or an honest "that isn't in the documents". It runs entirely on company hardware.

On an 88-question test set it cites the right document 85.7% of the time and makes the right call on whether to answer or decline 90.6% of the time. Comparing five local models cut unsupported claims from 19.3% to 6.9%.

Python, FastAPI, Qdrant, Ollama, Docling, DuckDB, React

2025–2026 · ID cards and receipts

OCR Suite

Security guards used to type visitor details by hand, and drivers' fuel and toll receipts were copied out twice before reaching Finance. The ID-card reader fills in up to 15 fields from one photo, so check-in dropped from 159 seconds to about 5, saving roughly 54 staff hours from January to September 2026.

The receipt readers handle fuel and toll receipts for driver expenses. Moving the fuel model to ONNX Runtime cut each scan from 2.8 to 1.8 seconds. The ID-card service is open source on GitHub.

PaddleOCR, OpenCV, ONNX Runtime, Flask, FastAPI, React

2025 · Product matching

Cariproduk

Customer requests arrive as free text, photos or Excel lists. Cariproduk matches each line to the exact item in our catalog, and it knows which flanges are interchangeable across DIN, JIS and ANSI standards. In monthly checks through 2025 it stayed between 90% and 97.6% accurate, against an 80% target.

Gemini API with a local Llama fallback, FAISS, Meilisearch, FastAPI

2026 · Built in four weeks

Sales Intelligence

Managers wanted to see the week's sales pipeline without digging through the CRM. I built a weekly report and a page for every customer. Rules decide every number and finding; a local language model only writes the sentences. After an accuracy audit, 22 of 23 summary points checked out.

Python, Qwen 2.5 (local), scheduled jobs, React

2025–2026 · Procurement

Demand forecasting

Most of our ~11,000 products sell rarely and unevenly, which breaks standard forecasts. I grouped them by demand pattern, tested intermittent-demand models such as Croston and ADIDA with rolling backtests, and gave procurement seven analyses for deciding what to reorder and when, including lead times per supplier.

StatsForecast, LightGBM, Polars, DuckDB, MLflow, Streamlit
